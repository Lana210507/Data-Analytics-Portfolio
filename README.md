## 02 — TokoBli Campaign Performance & A/B Testing

**Focus:** EDA | Campaign Analytics | A/B Testing | Business Recommendations

An e-commerce campaign analysis evaluating the performance of the 10/10, 11/11, and 12/12 promotional campaigns, followed by an A/B test comparing two product detail page designs.

### Key Questions

- Which campaign performed best across revenue, customers, transactions, and units sold?
- Which campaign generated the strongest revenue relative to discount spending?
- Which product categories performed strongest?
- Did the new product detail page increase average transaction value?

### Key Findings

- **11/11** generated the highest revenue at **Rp16.62B**.
- **11/11** had the highest customer count at **1,567**.
- **12/12** generated the highest transaction volume at **3,414 transactions**.
- **12/12** sold the highest number of units at **4,485**.
- **12/12** achieved the highest revenue-to-discount ratio at **58.54**.
- **Men's Fashion** was the strongest category across major sales metrics.
- The A/B test showed an approximately **11.3% higher average transaction value** for Group B.
- The A/B test result was statistically significant with **p < 0.001**.
